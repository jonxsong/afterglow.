# Afterglow for iPhone support

Use [Afterglow Issues](https://github.com/jonxsong/afterglow./issues) to report a broken feature or ask a question. Include the iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps you followed. Issues are public, so leave out private photos and personal information.

- If your city is missing, try its main municipality or a nearby city.
- If you prefer to pick a city manually, use **Change location** in the forecast menu. You can manage the location permission in iPhone Settings.
- If the network is unavailable, Afterglow can display the same evening’s previously saved forecast. A new city or a new evening needs a connection.
- The photo comparison works with an image available on your iPhone. The Photos app may need internet access to retrieve an item stored only in iCloud.

See the [iPhone app privacy policy](./PRIVACY.md).
