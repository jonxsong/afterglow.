# Afterglow for iPhone privacy policy

## Forecasts and location

You can use Afterglow by searching for a city. The app sends your search to Afterglow’s forecast service, which uses Open-Meteo to find matching cities. Afterglow requests location access only when you choose **Use current location**. It takes a single location reading, rounds the coordinates to two decimal places, and uses Apple’s location service to look up the approximate city. You can change the permission in iPhone Settings at any time.

Afterglow’s forecast service receives the selected place and its coordinates to retrieve weather from Open-Meteo or 7Timer. The service keeps shared weather snapshots indexed by forecast area to handle provider outages. It can serve these snapshots for up to 12 hours. They contain weather data and an area key, with no account or device identifier. Cloudflare hosts the forecast service and processes its network requests.

The app saves the selected city and most recent forecast on your iPhone. Change the city to use a different location. Deleting the app removes its local preferences and forecast.

## Sunset photos

Choose one image through the iOS photo picker. Afterglow processes it on your iPhone to compare its visible colors with the forecast. A downscaled preview and color comparison stay in app memory for that session. Closing the comparison clears them. Afterglow does not upload the photo or its metadata, and does not alter the original in Photos.

## Accounts and tracking

Afterglow for iPhone does not require an account, include advertising, or use tracking SDKs. The public Afterglow website has separate features and this policy describes the iPhone app.
