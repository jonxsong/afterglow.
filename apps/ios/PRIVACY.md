# Afterglow for iPhone and Mac privacy policy

## Forecasts and location

You can use Afterglow by searching for a city. The app sends your search to Afterglow’s forecast service, which uses Open-Meteo to find matching cities. Afterglow requests location access only when you choose **Use current location**. It takes a single location reading, rounds the coordinates to two decimal places, and uses Apple’s location service to look up the approximate city. You can change the permission in iPhone Settings or Mac System Settings.

Afterglow’s forecast service receives the selected place and its coordinates to retrieve weather from Open-Meteo or 7Timer. The service keeps shared weather snapshots indexed by forecast area to handle provider outages. It can serve these snapshots for up to 12 hours. They contain weather data and an area key, with no account or device identifier. Cloudflare hosts the forecast service and processes its network requests.

The app saves the selected city and most recent forecast on the device you use. Change the city to use a different location.

## Sunset photos

Choose one image from Photos on iPhone or from Finder on Mac. Afterglow processes it on your device to compare its visible colors with the forecast. A downscaled preview and color comparison stay in app memory for that session. Closing the comparison clears them. The app leaves the original untouched and keeps the photo and its metadata on your device.

## Accounts and tracking

The iPhone and Mac apps work without an account, advertising, or tracking SDKs. This policy covers those two apps.
